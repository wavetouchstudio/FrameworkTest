**CoZno’s Crew** is a small group of unusually intelligent animals living beneath an abandoned hydroelectric dam, led by the outcast inventor **CoZno**. Using salvaged Old World technology and neural harnesses that allow animals to communicate and operate human machinery, the crew explores the dam and its surrounding infrastructure.

Each member has different physical strengths and approaches to problems, allowing them to access routes and mechanisms that others cannot. Together, they uncover the truth behind the dam’s failing power system and the growing **Steam Regime** above it.

What begins as survival and exploration becomes a larger mission: restore the dam, expose the manufactured water shortage, and stop the machinery being built to monopolize power and spread the Steam Regime beyond the valley.
